Speaking topics

Four signature talks.

Keynote · Fulfilment
Some Achieve Everything. Few Feel Fulfilled.

The signature talk. Joel explores why success and fulfilment are not the same thing — and what it actually takes to build a life that feels as good as it looks. Honest, researched, and deeply human.

Keynote · Identity
Who Are You When the Roles Are Gone?

A talk on identity, belonging, and the courage it takes to know yourself beyond your title, your achievements, and the expectations of others. For leaders, professionals, and anyone in transition.

Workshop · Seven Areas
The Seven Areas of Human Life

An interactive workshop guiding participants through a live version of the Fulfilment Diagnostic — and what the seven areas of life reveal about where they are and where they want to go.

Keynote · Emotional Intelligence
Feeling Your Way Forward

A talk on emotional intelligence, self-awareness, and why the most important conversations we will ever have are the ones we have with ourselves. Practical, evidence-based, and emotionally honest.
