What is Discover You?

More than a podcast.
A platform for growth.

The Discover You Podcast is a platform for authentic conversations that explore what it truly means to live a fulfilling life.

Through stories of challenge, growth, leadership, entrepreneurship, mental health, faith, and purpose, we help people reconnect with who they are and what matters most.

Every guest brings unique experiences, lessons, and insights that inspire others to grow, overcome obstacles, and create meaningful lives.

Meet the Host

Joel Benesha

Joel Benesha is an entrepreneur, speaker, coach, software engineer, and founder of Reality Creation Centre. Through the Discover You Podcast, Joel creates meaningful conversations that help people uncover purpose, fulfilment, resilience, and personal growth.

His mission is simple: give every person access to the conversations, tools, and community they need to build a life that truly means something.
