Reality Creation is a storytelling company based in Brisbane, Australia — helping founders, businesses, and organisations build trust through powerful stories.

We believe every person, brand, and organisation has a story worth telling well. Told with intention, that story becomes the foundation of trust — and trust is what drives real transformation, growth, and connection.

The Founder
Joel Benesha — Engineer. Podcaster. Coach. Storyteller.
"I built this because I needed it to exist. I looked for a place that held the whole person — not just the career, not just the mindset, but all seven areas at once. It didn't exist. So I built it."

Joel started as an engineer. He became a podcast host with 500,000+ views across all social platforms. Then a fulfilment coach. Then the founder of something bigger than any one role — a platform built on the belief that every person deserves a space to understand their whole life.

Reality Creation was not built as a personal brand. It was built as a community. Joel is its founder and guide — but it belongs to everyone who walks through it.
